4. Bank account access (PSD2 / Account Information Service)

When you connect a bank account, you are authorising read-only access to your account information through Enable Banking, a licensed Account Information Service Provider (AISP), under the EU’s PSD2 framework. Key points:

  • Access is granted by your explicit consent, given directly to your bank via its secure authentication (Strong Customer Authentication).
  • Consent is time-limited (typically up to ~180 days) and you can withdraw it at any time — from within the Service (disconnect) or directly with your bank.
  • We only read balances and transactions. We cannot and do not initiate payments or move money.

Connecting through open-banking.io (optional). Instead of connecting your bank directly, you can use your own account at open-banking.io, operated by Tatic ApS (Denmark), which provides account information under the licence of Enable Banking. In that case you give your bank consent in open-banking.io’s app, and you upload to the Service a credentials file you export from open-banking.io. That file contains a key that decrypts your account data: we store it encrypted, use it only to read your balances and transactions, and delete it when you disconnect. Your relationship with open-banking.io (including its fees and its own privacy policy) is between you and open-banking.io; to fully withdraw access, also remove the connection in open-banking.io’s app.

5. Automated classification (transaction text sent to Anthropic)

To suggest budget categories, the text of your transactions (such as the description, merchant and counterparty name) may be sent to Anthropic (the provider of the “Claude” AI model) for classification. This processing produces a suggested category only; it is not used to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you. We do not send your name, login credentials or full account numbers for this purpose beyond what appears in the transaction text itself.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep your account and financial data for as long as your account is active. If you close your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain certain records to comply with legal obligations. Temporary data (such as 2FA codes) is auto-expired within minutes.

8. How we protect your data

  • Sensitive secrets (such as 2FA secrets and bank-consent tokens) are encrypted at rest (AES-256); passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
  • Access is protected by authentication, two-factor authentication and rate limiting.
  • Application infrastructure is private and not exposed to the public internet beyond what is strictly necessary to operate the Service.
